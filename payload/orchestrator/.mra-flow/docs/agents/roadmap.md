# Initiative roadmaps

An **initiative** is an effort too large for one change. Its **roadmap**, `docs/roadmap/<initiative>.md`, cuts it into **slices**, one future change each, with an estimate per slice and a total, all in **effective hours** (ADR-0011 of mra-flow). Next to it, `docs/roadmap/<initiative>-effort.md` is generated by `.mra-flow/bin/mra-effort report <initiative>`: estimate vs. actual per slice and hours per phase; never edit it by hand. `/mra-roadmap` writes and revises it (ADR-0010 of mra-flow). It is optional: when `docs/roadmap/` does not exist, or no slice matches a change id, every roadmap step in the other skills is skipped silently.

## One initiative per project

An orchestrator has **one** initiative, named after the project: the orchestrator repo's directory name (`basename "$(git rev-parse --show-toplevel)"`), so its roadmap is `docs/roadmap/<project>.md` and its label `roadmap:<project>`. Every change is a slice of it, done, in progress and planned alike, so one effort file shows them all with their estimates (ADR-0012 of mra-flow).

- A new effort (a new topic for `/mra-roadmap`, a change found too large) adds slices to that roadmap: revise mode, never a second roadmap.
- Any other `docs/roadmap/*.md` (an initiative with another name, from before this rule) is merged into the project's roadmap by `/mra-roadmap`: its slices are appended after the existing ones and renumbered (their *Depends on* too), its Destination, Scope, constraints, Decisions, Assumptions and Risks join the project's, its Log lines are carried with a `(roadmap <name>, merged here)` mark, its file is removed; in beads its slice epics are reparented under the project's initiative epic, every issue labelled `roadmap:<name>` is relabelled `roadmap:<project>`, and its initiative epic is closed with `--reason "merged into initiative <project>"`.
- The project's initiative epic is the parent of **every** change epic, archived ones included, so `bd epic status <initiative epic>` counts all of them.

## A roadmap in days

Roadmaps written before ADR-0011 hold their estimate, revised and actual columns in person-days; `mra-effort` refuses them. `/mra-roadmap` converts one in place (or while merging it): the columns become hours, the size scale becomes the hour scale, and every open slice is **re-estimated** in effective hours with the done slices as reference class (never person-days × 8: one person-day was far more than the effective hours of a slice). The day ranges are kept in one Log line (`<date> (days) <change-id> <size> <range> d, ...`), so the original estimate is not lost; the re-estimate counts as the original from then on.

## Finding the slice of a change

A change belongs to a roadmap when a row of the **Slices** table of some `docs/roadmap/*.md` has that change id in its *Change id* column. A change id appears in at most one roadmap.

## A roadmap found missing late

A change that belongs to no roadmap may turn out, once proposed or archived, to be part of a larger effort. Two phases look for it and recommend `/mra-roadmap --retro` when the project has no roadmap yet, or `/mra-roadmap <project>` (revise: add the change and the rest as slices) when it has one:

- `/mra-propose`: the change, estimated from `tasks.md` with the default size scale, is XL, or it touches more than one capability with a contract change; the change then enters the roadmap as a `proposed` slice and the rest becomes new slices.
- `/mra-archive`: the proposal or design names work left for later (follow-ups, a next change, out-of-scope items meant to be done), or other archived changes modify the same capabilities; list those changes as candidate done slices.

The recommendation is made **once per change** and never blocks the phase. When the user declines, record `bd update <epic> --append-notes "roadmap: declined (<reason>)"` when the epic exists (else say it in the close summary), and never ask again for that change; neither phase asks when a `roadmap: declined` note is on the epic.

## The file

```markdown
---
initiative: <initiative>          # kebab-case, also the beads label roadmap:<initiative>
title: <title>
status: active                    # active | done | dropped
epic: <initiative epic id>        # filled once the epic exists
created: <YYYY-MM-DD>
---

# <title>

## Destination

<one or two lines: what the world looks like when the initiative is done>

## Scope

- In: <...>
- Out: <... (why)>

## Actors and constraints

<roles involved; contracts, compatibility, environments, non-functional requirements that bind every slice>

## Decisions

<!-- pointers only: ADRs and glossary terms of this initiative, one line each -->
- ADR-NNNN <title>: <gist>

## Slicing rule

<how the work was cut, e.g. vertical slices, each with observable value and shippable on its own; hub before gens on a contract change>

## Size scale

| Size | Hours | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| S | 2–4 | one repo, no contract change, no new screen |
| M | 4–8 | one or two repos, or one or two new screens, or a small optional contract addition |
| L | 8–14 | several repos with a contract change, or a new pattern (lists, upload, role-scoped editing), or a new capability |
| XL | > 14 | too large for one change: split it |

## Slices

| # | Change id | Goal | Repos | Contract | Depends on | UI preview | Size | Estimate (h) | Confidence | Revised (h) | Status | Epic | Start (UTC) | End (UTC) | Actual (h) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | add-foo | <one line> | hub | yes | – | prototype docs/prototypes/foo-screen.md | M | 5–8 | medium | | planned | bd-12 | | | |

<!-- "## Actuals by phase": written by `mra-effort record` (see Actuals below); never write the heading by hand -->

## Total

<written by `mra-effort report`, see Totals below>

## Assumptions

- <what the estimates rely on>

## Risks and open points

- [slice <#>] <open point the per-change /mra-grill must settle>
- [all] <cross-cutting risk>

## Log

- <YYYY-MM-DD> <what changed in the roadmap and why>
```

Column rules:

- **Change id**: kebab-case, the id `/mra-propose` will use. Stable once written: a renamed slice keeps its row and logs the rename.
- **Contract**: `yes` when the slice changes an interface between a repo and one of its `depends_on`.
- **Depends on**: slice numbers that must be archived first.
- **UI preview**: what the slice's screens need, per `.mra-flow/docs/agents/ui-preview.md`; one value per slice, the strongest when its screens differ:
  - `prototype` — needed, to be done in the slice's `/mra-grill`;
  - `prototype docs/prototypes/<slug>.md` — already done (in `/mra-roadmap` or a previous slice): the slice's `/mra-grill` reuses the verdict instead of prototyping again;
  - `sketch` — only changes to existing screens: a sketch in the slice's `/mra-grill`;
  - `–` — no screen in a repo's `ui_paths`.
  The final `preview:` line of each screen (`.mra-flow/docs/agents/ui-preview.md`, *How it is recorded*) is still written by the slice's `/mra-grill` and lands in `design.md`, not here.
- **Estimate (h)**: the original range in effective hours (one developer working with the agent, from `/mra-grill` to `/mra-archive`, hotfixes of the slice included). Written once; never rewritten, so it stays comparable with the actual. The variance report compares the actual with its **upper bound**.
- **Confidence**: `high` (similar done slices exist, no open points), `medium`, `low` (open points or unknown technology; widen the range), or `retro`: a done slice estimated after the work by `--retro` (see *Retro estimates*).
- **Revised (h)**: the range after `/mra-propose`, from `tasks.md`. Empty until then.
- **Status**: `planned` → `proposed` (`/mra-propose`) → `in progress` (`/mra-track`) → `done` (`/mra-archive`); or `dropped` (by `/mra-roadmap` only, with a log line).
- **Start (UTC)**, **End (UTC)**: the first and the last minute of work on the slice, `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM` (typically in its `/mra-grill` and its `/mra-archive`), from `mra-effort measure` (`start`, `end`): Start written by `/mra-track`, both by `/mra-archive` and `--retro`, always through `mra-effort record --start/--end`. A roadmap without these columns gets them from the first `record` that sets a date.
- **Actual (h)**: effective hours spent, written by `mra-effort record` in `/mra-archive` (see Actuals).

## Totals

The **Total** section holds three lines, four once a slice has a date. Never compute them by hand: whoever changes a size, estimate, status or actual runs `.mra-flow/bin/mra-effort report <initiative>`, which rewrites them and regenerates `<initiative>-effort.md`:

- `Estimate: <min>–<max> h` — the sum of the original estimates of all slices not dropped.
- `Forecast: <min>–<max> h` — the sum, over slices not dropped, of the actual for done slices, else the revised range, else the original one.
- `Progress: <done>/<total> slices; actual <sum of actuals> h vs. estimated <sum of the original ranges of the done slices> h`.
- `Period: <start> → <end> (UTC)` — when the project's work began and ended: the earliest *Start* of its slices to the latest *End*, `in progress` until every slice not dropped is done; absent while no slice has a date. The effort file repeats it under its title.

The effort file holds one table, **Variance by slice**: per slice not dropped, its epic, change id, goal, start and end, estimate range (*Estimate (h)*), estimated hours (its upper bound), actual hours, variance (actual − estimated) in hours and in percent of the estimate, and 🔴 when over the estimate, 🟢 when within it, ⚪ when not done; then one column per phase used (`mra-grill`, `mra-prototype`, `mra-propose`, … `mra-hotfix`), with the actual hours of that phase from *Actuals by phase*. A **Total** row sums the done slices; its variance covers the slices that have both an estimate and an actual.

## Actuals

The actual is **effective hours**: the time the agent or the user was active on the change, not days elapsed. `.mra-flow/bin/mra-effort measure <change-id>` computes it from this orchestrator's Claude Code session logs (`$CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` or `~/.claude`, `projects/<orchestrator path with every non-alphanumeric character as ->/`, each session with its subagents):

- an interval between two timestamped events of a session counts when it is at most 10 minutes long (`--gap`); longer pauses (nights, a suspended laptop, waiting on the user) are left out;
- it goes to the phase of the `/mra-*` command in progress in that session (`mra-grill` → grill, `mra-verify` and `mra-qa-ui` → verify, …) when the command's arguments name the change id, or an `--alias` (a grill topic, a hotfix's bd id);
- stretches in the change's period (from a day before its first attributed minute to its last) that no command attributes, or whose command names no known change, are listed with session, time, duration and the last prompt, to be assigned to a phase or ignored;
- parallel sessions are summed, not merged;
- `start` and `end` (JSON; `from … to … UTC` in the text) are the first and last attributed minute: the slice's *Start* and *End*.

In `--retro`, collect the aliases and the stretches from the logs before asking, so the user confirms one figure per change instead of rebuilding it:

- **aliases**: every `/mra-grill`, `/mra-research`, `/mra-prototype` and `/mra-hotfix` whose arguments name no change id, in the unattributed stretches of a change's period (`measure --json`, field `unattributed`, `context`), becomes an `--alias` of the change its topic belongs to (the change whose proposal it led to, or whose capability it touched); a hotfix's bd id becomes an alias of the change whose feature it fixed;
- **commands without a change id**: an unattributed `/mra-build`, `/mra-fix`, `/mra-verify` or `/mra-archive` stretch goes to the change whose bd issues were claimed or closed inside it (`bd list --parent <epic> --all --json`, `closed_at`), at that command's phase;
- a resumed session with no command (e.g. "let's resume the grilling") goes to the phase it resumes when the previous command of that session names or is aliased to the change;
- everything else (work on the tooling, mra-flow itself, questions) is left out; list what was assigned and what was left out in the Assumptions.

`/mra-archive` runs it before integrating, shows the per-phase hours and the unattributed stretches, and asks the user to confirm or correct the figures; then `mra-effort record <initiative> <change-id> --phase <phase>=<hours> ...` writes *Actual (h)* and the row of *Actuals by phase*, and `mra-effort report <initiative>` refreshes the totals and the effort file. `--retro` measures each done change the same way (with `--alias` for its grill topic), and always asks.

## Retro estimates

A slice that `--retro` brings in as done has no original estimate, so the effort file could not compare it. `/mra-roadmap --retro` sizes it as it would size a planned slice, from its drivers (repos, contract, capabilities, screens of its proposal and specs) and the size scale, **without looking at its actual**, and writes Size, *Estimate (h)* and confidence `retro`. The effort file then shows its variance (🔴/🟢); a note in Assumptions says these estimates calibrate the scale and are not forecasts. When the retro actuals sit well under or over the scale, step 7 sizes the open slices from those actuals, and says so.

## Beads

- **Initiative epic**: `bd create "<initiative>: <title>" -t epic -l roadmap:<initiative> --spec-id docs/roadmap/<initiative>.md -d "<Destination>"`. From `--from <map-epic>`, the wayfinder map epic is reused instead: `bd update <map-epic> --add-label roadmap:<initiative> --spec-id docs/roadmap/<initiative>.md`.
- **Slice epic**: one child per slice not done, `bd create "<change-id>: <goal>" -t epic --parent <initiative epic> -d "<goal; open points of the slice>" --metadata '{"slice":"<change-id>"}' --estimate <minutes>`, the estimate being the upper bound of *Estimate (h)* × 60; it inherits `roadmap:<initiative>`. Find it with `bd list --parent <initiative epic> -t epic --metadata-field slice=<change-id> --all`.
- **Promotion**: `/mra-track` turns the slice epic into the change epic (`bd update <slice epic> --add-label openspec:<change-id> --spec-id openspec/changes/<change-id> --title "<change-id>: <proposal title>" -d "<proposal Why>"`) instead of creating a new one; tasks become its children as usual.
- **Estimate on the epic**: the slice epic's `--estimate` (minutes) always holds the upper bound of the slice's current range: set at creation by `/mra-roadmap`, updated to the upper bound of *Revised (h)* by `/mra-propose` (`bd update <slice epic> --estimate <minutes>`), and in revise mode whenever an open slice's estimate changes. `bd show` and `bd list` then show it next to the work.
- **Order**: for each `Depends on`, `bd dep add <slice epic> <slice epic it depends on>`.
- **Existing change epics** (`--retro`, archived and in flight alike): `bd update <change epic> --parent <initiative epic> --add-label roadmap:<initiative> --metadata '{"slice":"<change-id>"}'`, and `--estimate <upper bound × 60>` for one not done; archived changes get no new epic: their row records the old epic id, if any. When every slice is done, the initiative epic is closed; a later revise that adds slices reopens it (`bd reopen`) and sets the front matter back to `status: active`.
- **Dropped slice**: `bd close <slice epic> --reason "dropped from roadmap <initiative>: <why>"`.
- **Done initiative**: when every slice is done or dropped, `/mra-archive` offers `bd close <initiative epic>` and sets the front matter `status: done`.

## Who writes what

| Skill | Writes |
|---|---|
| `/mra-roadmap` | everything: rows, sizes, original and retro estimates, scale, assumptions, risks, epics, log; merges other roadmaps and converts day roadmaps |
| `/mra-grill` | nothing; reports **roadmap impact** in its close summary and recommends `/mra-roadmap <initiative>` when slices must change |
| `/mra-propose` | its row: Revised, Status `proposed`; the slice epic's estimate; Total and effort file (`mra-effort report`); a log line when the revised range leaves the original one |
| `/mra-track` | its row: Status `in progress`, Epic; promotes the slice epic |
| `/mra-archive` | its row: Status `done`, Actual and Actuals by phase (`mra-effort record`); Total and effort file (`mra-effort report`); a log line; closes the initiative when done |
